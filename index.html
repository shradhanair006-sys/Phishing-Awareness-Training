What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which attackers pretend to be trusted sources to steal sensitive information such as passwords and banking details.

How to Identify Phishing Emails

  • Check for spelling and grammar mistakes.
  • Look for suspicious sender email addresses.
  • Be careful with unexpected attachments.
  • Avoid clicking unknown links.
  • Watch out for urgent messages asking for personal details.

Common Social Engineering Tricks

  • Fake emails pretending to be from banks.
  • Messages claiming that your account will be blocked.
  • Fake lottery or prize-winning messages.
  • Impersonation of trusted people or organizations.

Safety Tips to Avoid Phishing

  • Never share your passwords or OTP.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Verify links before clicking.
  • Report suspicious emails.

How to Identify Fake Websites

  • Check whether the website uses HTTPS.
  • Look carefully for spelling mistakes in the website URL.
  • Be careful of websites asking for unnecessary personal information.
  • Check whether the website has genuine contact details.
  • Avoid websites offering unbelievable discounts or prizes.
